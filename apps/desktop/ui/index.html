Driver, buffer and — under ASIO — the device restart the capture engine, the way a DAW does when the driver changes: the phones reconnect on their own. ASIO loads a kernel-mode driver: an unstable one can restart the whole machine, so it is never chosen automatically — pick it deliberately, and prefer your interface's own driver over a built-in one. The buffer only takes effect on ASIO, where the driver honours it; WASAPI shared mode pins the period to the device's own (10 ms). The sample rate is 48 kHz by design: it is the format on the wire the phones receive, not a free choice.

Advanced: add a musician by address

For a fixed rack or for debugging. Adding or removing a musician never restarts the server: the capture stream, the control server and the discovery beacon keep running. Removing stops that musician's audio immediately; their control channel stays open and their next mix is answered with "no UDP target configured for client IP".
