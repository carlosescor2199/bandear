# Shared redesign (iOS + Android)

Objective: one minimal look for both clients, on the Android brand, with a mixing-console Mix screen and every screen in portrait and landscape. Branch `feat/shared-redesign` (on top of `feat/ios-client`). Delivery: `ask-on-risk`, chain strategy `stacked-to-main` (L11). Runners: `swift test --package-path apps/ios/BandEarKit` + `xcodebuild` (iOS); `gradle -p apps/android :app:testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` (Android, toolchain missing on this Mac, see L6).

Reference design: the Design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/YZKHchMMTaV4kKfxfuWH3t (private). Boards: Connect, Mix, Channel panel, Mixes and Settings, each "· portrait" (390×844) and "· landscape" (844×390; Settings 844×620), plus "Design system". The canvas sources are the `.dc.html` files of that artifact; the board names below refer to them.

## Specs

- **S1 Brand.** Both apps use the Android brand tokens: the colours of `BandEarDarkColors` (`apps/android/.../ui/theme/Color.kt`), IBM Plex Sans for text and JetBrains Mono for numbers (`Type.kt`, the variable TTFs in `res/font` with their OFL texts in `assets/licenses`), the radii of `Shape.kt` (6 tag, 8 key, 10 control, 12 card, 14 panel, 22 pill). Dark only. iOS gets a theme that mirrors these values one to one and bundles the same fonts and licence texts.
- **S2 Mix console.** As the boards "Mix · portrait" and "Mix · landscape": channel strips side by side, each, top to bottom: the M and S keys (a held strip shows its "Locked" or "Following" tag there instead); a small horizontal pan with a centre mark and a mono readout (`panText`), editable on the strip through the existing pan action; a vertical fader with a console cap, tick marks and a 4 pt/dp track (fill `faderIdle`, the accent on the musician's own channel, `faderMuted` when muted; a held strip is dimmed and takes no input); the value text (`stripValueText`); a "scribble strip" button with the label and the group name (or "No group"), on the group's tone (`groupToneFor`), the accent tint on the musician's own channel, which opens the channel panel (S4). The master is a strip pinned at the right: "MASTER", the Mute key, a vertical fader with a marker at the engineer's limit when there is one, the value, and a bottom button showing the level cap that opens the master panel (S5). Header: musician name and server, the connection pill; under it More of me (S6) and the Channels/Groups segmented control. Portrait: the channel strips scroll horizontally and the master stays pinned. Landscape: the same row with all strips that fit, under a one-line header.
- **S3 Group strips.** Same strip shell, level only (groups have no mute, solo or pan today and this redesign adds none): fader, value, a scribble strip on the group's tone with the group name and `groupMembersText`, the "Following" tag where it applies.
- **S4 Channel panel.** The content of today's channel sheet (header, held notes, pan, level, Mute/Solo, "Done"), presented as a bottom sheet in portrait and as a panel from the right edge in landscape (boards "Channel panel · portrait/landscape").
- **S5 Master panel (assumption).** The level cap stays editable: the master strip's bottom button opens a panel, in the same container as S4, with the level-cap slider, the existing line "Level cap " + "N%" + " · protects your ears" and the existing engineer-limit text. The canvas does not draw this panel; it follows the channel panel's look.
- **S6 More of me (assumption).** A compact switch in the header area, with the "My channel" picker as a menu button beside it showing the chosen channel; the caption (`moreOfMeCaption`) becomes the switch's accessibility value; the notice stays a live-region line under the header.
- **S7 Navigation.** Portrait: the bottom tab bar (Mix, Mixes, Settings) on the bar colour, the selected tab in the accent. Landscape: a left rail with the same three tabs. Connect shows neither.
- **S8 Other screens.** Connect, Mixes and Settings restyled as their boards; in landscape Connect splits into identity | servers, Mixes into save | list, Settings into connection | diagnostics.
- **S9 Copy.** Every existing copy helper and accessibility string keeps its output (they are pinned by the Android `*TextTest`s and the iOS `*ParityTests`). The only new string is "Swipe for more", shown beside "CHANNELS · N" in portrait when the strips overflow; it is added to both apps' copy helpers with a parity test.
- **S10 Orientation.** Phones in portrait and landscape on both platforms (already allowed by the manifest and the iOS build settings); the layout follows the current orientation, and state survives rotation.
- **S11 Accessibility.** Every control keeps the labels, values, hints and actions it has today (strip fader, M/S keys, label button, group fader, master fader, Mute master, level cap, pan, picker, pill, the VoiceOver/TalkBack notices); new controls (strip pan) reuse the existing pan wording.
- **S12 Scope.** No new protocol, state or actions; iOS stays on its iOS 16 deployment target (the rail and the side panel are custom views); BandEarKit stays free of SwiftUI/UIKit.
