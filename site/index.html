How it works

From your mixer to every pair of ears in three steps

  1. 1

    Connect your mixer

    Most digital mixers have a USB audio interface built in — BandEar uses it. A standalone audio interface works too. Plug it into a Windows, macOS or Linux computer, open BandEar, pick it and press Start engine.

  2. 2

    Run the desk

    The desktop app builds one mix per musician with channel processing, limits and presets. No mixer at hand? The demo source plays eight generated channels.

  3. 3

    Connect the phones

    Musicians join the same Wi-Fi and open the BandEar app. Their phones find the desk on their own — no IP addresses to type — and everyone mixes their own ears.

Features

A real monitor desk, without the extra hardware

Personal mixes

  • One mix per musician, from their phone or the desk
  • Up to 32 inputs, stereo pairs and groups
  • Engineer limits and locks, presets, copy and mirror mixes
  • Saved sessions and automatic mix backups

Processing

  • High-pass, 4-band EQ, gate and compressor on every input
  • A limiter on every musician's master
  • Shared reverb and delay with sends and per-mix returns
  • 0 ms of added latency

Recording

  • Multitrack: every input on its own WAV track
  • Markers, disk-space estimate and crash-safe files
  • Virtual soundcheck: play a take back as if the band were playing
  • A local mix for the computer's own outputs

Network you can trust

  • Link health per musician: loss, glitches and round trip
  • Set each phone's buffer from the desk
  • Redundancy: every packet sent twice when Wi-Fi gets rough
  • Engine load meter, log file and a demo source

FAQ

Questions musicians ask

Is it free?

Yes. BandEar is free and open source under the MIT license — no subscriptions, no accounts, no ads.

Do I need special hardware?

No. You need a computer, a mixer with USB audio (or any audio interface the computer sees as an input) and a Wi-Fi network. Musicians use their own phones and in-ear headphones. A dedicated 5 GHz access point close to the stage works best.

Which phones work?

Android 10 or newer. Each phone finds the desk on the same Wi-Fi by itself.

What about latency?

Processing adds 0 ms: filters and dynamics have no look-ahead and effects are wet returns. The engine builds every mix once per 5.3 ms block, and with 32 inputs and 16 musicians it uses about 18% of each block. The total delay you hear depends on your Wi-Fi and each phone's buffer, which you can set from the desk.

Is there an iOS app?

Not yet — an iOS app for musicians is on the roadmap.

Why does my computer show a security warning?

The installers are not signed yet. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway; on macOS right-click the app and choose Open the first time. Signed installers are on the roadmap.
